Da sua: src/main/webapp/WEB-INF/jspf/common.jspf
- Ham esc(String) truoc do co chuoi Java sai cu phap: replace(""", ...)
- Da sua thanh replace("\"", ...)

HUONG DAN:
1. Mo thu muc RestMan_2_Module (co pom.xml) trong VS Code.
2. Chay: mvn clean package
3. Dung Tomcat neu dang chay. Xoa webapps/resmain va webapps/resmain.war cu de tranh tai nguyen trien khai cu.
4. Copy target/resmain.war vao D:\apache-tomcat-9.0.115\webapps\resmain.war
5. Khoi dong Tomcat va mo http://localhost:8080/resmain/

Luu y: Maven BUILD SUCCESS khong tu dong kiem tra bien dich JSP tren Tomcat.
Ban nay da sua loi cu the tu anh bao loi; chua duoc kiem thu thuc te tren Tomcat cua ban.
